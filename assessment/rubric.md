# British Library Source-Lens Dossier — analytic rubric

| Criterion | Outcome | 1 Beginning | 2 Developing | 3 Secure | 4 Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Terminology and question focus | AO1 / DS-U01-001 | Terms remain vague or the response drifts from the question. | Relevant terms appear, but the exact demand is only partly addressed. | Precise terminology sustains a clear answer to the exact question. | Terminology supports a nuanced interpretation and remains controlled throughout. |
| Technical mechanism | AO2 / DS-U02-006 / U03-004 | Technology is named without explaining how it causes an effect. | Some stages appear, but causal links remain general or incomplete. | An accurate system chain explains how a decision produces stakeholder consequences. | The mechanism identifies decision points, uncertainty and where safeguards alter the chain. |
| Evidence and source use | DS-IQ-003 | Claims lack relevant evidence or misrepresent a source. | Relevant evidence appears, but origin or limitation receives little attention. | Specific evidence supports claims and source purpose/limits are considered. | Evidence is triangulated; sources are judged as useful for particular claims rather than ranked absolutely. |
| Stakeholders and 3Cs | DS-U01-002 / IQ-004 | Stakeholders or course lenses are listed without analysis. | One perspective and concept connect loosely to the case. | Distinct stakeholders and selected 3Cs shape the analysis. | Connections between mechanism, stakeholders and 3Cs reveal unequal effects and competing priorities. |
| Evaluation and judgement | AO3 / DS-IQ-005 | The response describes or makes unsupported extreme claims. | A counterpoint appears, but the conclusion mainly summarizes. | Impacts and implications are distinguished; a trade-off supports a conditional judgement. | The judgement weighs significance, timeframe, probability and uncertainty while identifying what evidence could change it. |
