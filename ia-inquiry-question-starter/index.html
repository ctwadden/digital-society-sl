BUILD YOUR INQUIRY QUESTION

Today you are not doing the research. You are developing two possible inquiry questions that could grow into a strong IA.

What are we trying to make?

A strong inquiry question is focused, researchable, open-ended and connected to a real digital-society issue. It should be possible to examine it from more than one perspective.

REAL-WORLD ISSUEA current event, policy, platform, system, case or community.
→
DIGITAL SYSTEMWhat technology or digital system is involved?
→
PEOPLE / COMMUNITYWho is affected, helped, harmed, influenced or given power?
→
OPEN QUESTIONA question worth investigating — not a simple yes/no.
REAL ISSUE + DIGITAL SYSTEM + PEOPLE + DEBATABLE QUESTION = A GOOD START

How should I word it?

✓ Better wording

  • To what extent...
  • How far...
  • In what ways...
  • How significant...

Your wording should invite evidence, different perspectives and a judgement.

✕ Avoid

  • Yes / no questions
  • Huge topics such as “Is AI bad?”
  • Vague words such as “good”, “easy” or “better”
  • A question where the answer is already obvious
Too broad: Is social media bad for teenagers?
More useful: To what extent do age-verification systems on social-media platforms protect teenagers while creating new privacy concerns?

The 3Cs check

You do not need to write your IA today. You only need to show that your question has a possible connection to all three parts of Digital Society.

C

Concept

What idea could help you examine the issue?

Examples: Power, Values & Ethics, Identity, Change, Systems...

C

Content

What digital system or technology is at the centre?

Examples: AI, algorithms, social media, data, networks, robotics...

C

Context

Where is the issue happening in society?

Examples: social, political, economic, cultural, health, education...

Is it really a real-world issue?

Before keeping a question, make sure you could eventually point to a specific real case.

☐ A real company, government, school, platform, community or organization is involved.

☐ Real people or communities are affected.
☐ There could be more than one reasonable perspective.
☐ The issue is focused enough to investigate.
☐ I can imagine a specific real-world example connected to it.
