# Floor Shift — Computer Advice Lab

Interview a fictional client, compare two computers and the complete setup cost, then explain your decision. Preserve the first recommendation, use the feedback to revise, and test a changed brief.

The component workbench includes desktop/laptop 3D cutaways and a text guide. All components, prices and workload profiles are authored teaching examples, not retail quotations or measured benchmarks. No purchases or paid AI services are used. Three.js r149 and its MIT licence are bundled locally.

## Save and submit

Work saves in this browser on this device. Download the JSON backup before leaving a shared computer. The CSV report preserves raw fit, explanations, revisions and support context. Neither download submits work to school.

In Shift report, expand a recorded quote and choose **Prepare Google submission**. Copy the complete report, open the linked school Form, choose **Submit adviser quote**, paste into **IBDS-FS-QUOTE — Paste the prepared adviser quote**, and press **Submit** in Google. The Google confirmation is the submission receipt. The Form requires the eligible school account; a name or learner ID in the app cannot change that identity. Submit the first quote and its changed-brief quote separately when your teacher asks.

The same Form also contains **Knowledge + reflection**, covering the eight taught ideas and the four skill-reflection questions.

Raw fit /12 is automated model feedback, not an IB grade. Teacher review determines achievement. Supports and selected practice/independent modes do not establish a teacher-approved result. No automatic PowerSchool or predicted-grade write occurs.

## Run locally

Serve this folder through a local HTTP server. Open the server URL in a modern browser; opening index.html as a file does not reliably support modules. No build step or dependency installation is needed.
