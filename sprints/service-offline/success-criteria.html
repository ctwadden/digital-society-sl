What successful thinking looks like

These criteria describe quality, not answers. The independent task uses a hospital case you have not seen.

Independent transfer · /8 · 20 minutes

Command term: evaluate — weigh strengths and limitations and reach a supported judgment. Your teacher marks the whole response against the Paper 1 part c markband, using best fit. Naming stakeholders or concepts does not earn marks by itself.

A stronger response…Looks like…
tests the claim against the evidenceUses specific log entries to show what the claim explains and what it does not
explains the mechanism accuratelyNames the component or failure type precisely (e.g. server, network, name lookup, overload, malware)
offers a supported alternativeGives a better-fitting explanation and the evidence for it
traces the consequence for peopleLinks the failure to what patients and staff can no longer do, and why it matters
weighs a counter-perspectiveTakes another priority seriously before judging
reaches a supported judgmentStates a priority and what evidence would change it

Conditions: individual, closed-book, no AI or Coach, approved access arrangements apply. Feedback within five teaching days; a fresh retry on another new case follows.

Weekly quizzes · /12

Eight multiple choice (1 mark each) and two short responses (2 marks each). Six items cover the week’s reading and four revisit earlier learning. Quizzes are not converted to IB grades.

Synthesis (d07) and defence (d09)

Collected or observed for feedback, not scored. Aim for accurate claims, an explicit relationship, specific evidence, a limitation and an inference that stays within the evidence.
