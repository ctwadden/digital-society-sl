Independent chapter reading checks

Read and learn the assigned section, then show what you understand without notes. Your teacher assigns one chapter at a time. Four short responses take about six minutes; timing needs a classroom trial.

These are independent chapter checks for teacher feedback. They are separate from the registered Thursday /12 quiz. This local app keeps your work in this browser and exports it; it sends nothing to Google or Student OS. Knowledge evidence can show understanding of the reading, but cannot prove how you studied.
