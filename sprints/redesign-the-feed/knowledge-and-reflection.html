5 · Reflect and return to feedback

Use the workbook’s Journal hand-off or the chapter check’s reflection export. In Student OS open My blog, start an entry for IBDS and record:

  1. One thing you can now explain, with the evidence reference.
  2. A correction you made after feedback and the reading that supports it.
  3. The support used and the decision you made independently.
  4. One precise next target and a fresh task that could test it.

Save your draft. To send it to your teacher, choose the teacher audience and use Post to teacher. Keep it private unless the task calls for sharing. The short end-of-class exit note can name what clicked, what is still fuzzy and your confidence. Return through My progress or Review list for released feedback and corrections.

The existing Sprint 3 quiz/practice Forms do not supply a general chapter knowledge-and-reflection intake. Reflection goes through Student OS; the dedicated Google Form is a prepared local specification awaiting registration. School-account access and delivery still need a classroom check before rollout.
