Paper 1-style rehearsal · explain, analyse, evaluate

Original unscored practice, not an official IB examination. Use the event-notice scenario in Lab 2. Choose a fresh notice and trace its score and place in the order. Explain how input choice and an objective affect the result. Analyse how a changed ranking could affect two stakeholders, distinguishing a model result from an observed social consequence. Evaluate one possible redesign against a stated objective; discuss a trade-off, an evidence limit and a condition that could change your judgement.

Success: accurate operations → contextual consequences with support → reasoned conditional judgement. Use the preserved P1/P2 practice for the existing teacher-assigned questions and assessment route. No invented official mark maximum applies to this rehearsal.

Paper 2-style inquiry · sources in conversation

Original unscored source-based preparation. Source A: TikTok's provider explanation, June 2020, “What factors contribute to For You?”. Source B: European Commission preliminary finding, February 2026, “Risk assessment” and “Risk mitigation measures”. Read both originals; the dates and purposes differ.

  1. Identify one interaction signal in A and one design feature discussed in B. Give locators.
  2. Explain how a response can enter the next recommendation. Which source supports which part of the explanation?
  3. Compare what the provider account and the preliminary regulator finding can establish about user welfare. Discuss method, purpose and a limit; do not equate disagreement with falsehood.
  4. Evaluate whether one proposed design change could improve user agency. Use both sources, qualify any inference and state what further evidence would be needed.

Use a claim–source–explanation chain and bring sources together around the question, rather than writing two disconnected summaries. The Commission text is a preliminary finding, not a final determination. The 2020 provider text does not reveal today's exact ranking weights.
