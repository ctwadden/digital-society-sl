What successful thinking looks like

These criteria describe quality, not answers. The independent task on Wednesday uses a new case you have not seen.

Independent transfer · /8 · 20 minutes

Command term: evaluate — weigh strengths and limitations and reach a supported judgment. Your teacher marks the whole response against the Paper 1 part c markband (best fit). No marks come from simply naming stakeholders or concepts.

A stronger response…Looks like…
explains the mechanism accuratelySays exactly which weights change and what that does to scores
uses the dataUses at least one calculation or rank change from the table as evidence
weighs both sidesWho gains and who loses, and why that matters to them
names the valuesWhat the objective prioritizes, and what it gives up
judges conditionallyA conclusion with conditions or limits (when, for whom, with what safeguard)
is organizedA clear line of argument, not a list

Conditions: individual, closed-book, calculator allowed, no AI or Coach, approved access arrangements apply. Feedback within five teaching days; a fresh retry on another new case follows.

Weekly quizzes · /12

Eight multiple choice (1 mark each) and two short responses (2 marks each). Six items on the week’s reading, four revisiting earlier learning. Quizzes help you and your teacher find gaps. They are not converted to IB grades.

Source synthesis (d07) and defence (d09)

These are collected and observed for feedback. They are not scored. Aim for an accurate claim, an explicit relationship, specific evidence, a limitation and an inference that stays within the evidence.
