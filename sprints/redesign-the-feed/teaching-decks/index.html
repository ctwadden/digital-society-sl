Using a deck

Reading view shows the explanation on every slide. Listen, pause and resume are controlled by you. Presentation view enlarges the material for class. Checkpoints are practice with feedback. A wrong answer is a reason to revisit an explanation and try a new example.

The editable deck contains narration in its speaker notes. Use the browser version for read-aloud, simulations, links and the prediction gate. Text view gives an accessible study route. Your last slide is kept locally. Checkpoint choices and the short prediction are not stored or submitted.
