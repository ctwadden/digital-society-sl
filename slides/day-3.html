DS · Mechanism

How it actually works

A qualified judgement answers the degree of the question, identifies for whom and under what conditions, weighs the strongest counter-evidence and states what uncertainty limits the conclusion.

DS · Evidence

Checkpoint and exit

Checkpoint

The final response visibly integrates all seven Operation 7 gates, uses at least two attributed sources and ends with a conditional degree judgement.

Exit question

Which Operation 7 gate most improved your response, and what sentence proves it?

Speaker notes
