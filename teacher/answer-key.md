# Enter the Inquiry — answer key

## Knowledge check

1. **DS-U02-006** — The teacher-built framework uses DS-U02-006 for systems; U02-005 is space.
2. **Long disruption may change researcher trust if access remains uncertain** — An implication extends an impact into plausible longer-term significance and states a condition.
3. **It changes what the student notices or judges** — A useful concept changes the question, explanation or judgement.
4. **Useful for which claim and with what limitation** — Source usefulness depends on the claim, origin and limitation.
5. **For whom, under what conditions and with what limit** — High-level judgement remains evidence-based and qualified.

## Transfer and defence prompts

1. **Why is ransomware not a complete system definition?**
   - Listen for: The analysis must also identify people, data, infrastructure, rules and institutions through which the technology acts.
2. **What belongs on an uncertain causal arrow?**
   - Listen for: An inference or unknown label rather than invented certainty.
3. **When is a source useful?**
   - Listen for: When its origin and evidence make it suitable for a specific claim, with a named limitation.
4. **How does a stakeholder become analytical?**
   - Listen for: Name the stakeholder's role, interest, lost capability and source-backed consequence.
5. **How is an implication different from an impact?**
   - Listen for: An implication extends significance over time and states a condition or likelihood.
6. **What makes a counter-perspective genuine?**
   - Listen for: Evidence changes or narrows the original claim rather than merely presenting the opposite opinion.
7. **What belongs in a qualified judgement?**
   - Listen for: Degree, stakeholder, timeframe, conditions, counter-evidence and uncertainty.
8. **What does the defence prove?**
   - Listen for: That the student can trace the judgement through all seven gates to evidence.

Accept alternate wording when the student uses accurate evidence and can demonstrate the decision.
