## Three-class route

### Class 1: Operation 7 Gates 1–3: claim only what evidence supports

- 0–8: Open with the catalogue-going-dark scenario. Students distinguish what they observe, what they infer and what remains unknown.
- 8–20: Explain: Responsible analysis separates the technical chain from the evidence chain. Every causal arrow needs a source, an inference label or an explicit unknown marker.
- 20–35: Model the first three named steps and stop after each expected result.
- 35–60: Guided production/inquiry; use the warning and repair fields.
- 60–67: Checkpoint: Gates 1–3 are complete: precise system definition, supported/inferred/unknown mechanism map and three-source matrix with limitations.
- 67–70: Exit: Which arrow in your mechanism is strongest, which is uncertain and why?

### Class 2: Operation 7 Gates 4–6: analyse significance

- 0–8: Assign the same supported fact to four stakeholders and compare how the consequence changes.
- 8–20: Explain: The same technical event produces unequal consequences because stakeholders occupy different positions in the system. Evaluation tests the first claim against evidence that qualifies it and choices that sacrifice one value for another.
- 20–35: Model the first three named steps and stop after each expected result.
- 35–60: Guided production/inquiry; use the warning and repair fields.
- 60–67: Checkpoint: Gates 4–6 are complete for two distinct stakeholders; implications are conditional; counter-perspective and trade-off are evidence-based.
- 67–70: Exit: Which stakeholder difference most changes the extent of your claim?

### Class 3: Operation 7 Gate 7: judge, calibrate and defend

- 0–8: Compare weak, secure and Grade-7-oriented responses. Highlight the first sentence where the level changes and explain why.
- 8–20: Explain: A qualified judgement answers the degree of the question, identifies for whom and under what conditions, weighs the strongest counter-evidence and states what uncertainty limits the conclusion.
- 20–35: Model the first three named steps and stop after each expected result.
- 35–60: Guided production/inquiry; use the warning and repair fields.
- 60–67: Checkpoint: The final response visibly integrates all seven Operation 7 gates, uses at least two attributed sources and ends with a conditional degree judgement.
- 67–70: Exit: Which Operation 7 gate most improved your response, and what sentence proves it?

## Assessment boundary

The website records practice only. Confirm achievement by inspecting the required evidence, asking the defence questions and using the rubric.
