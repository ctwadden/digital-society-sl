# Enter the Inquiry - Source Notes

## Curriculum

The codes below come from the teacher-built SL Outcomes Framework v2. They support local planning and PowerSchool; they are not official IB codes.

- DS-U01-001: Explain digital society through impacts and implications of digital systems.
- DS-U01-002: Use concepts, content and contexts to organize inquiry.
- DS-U02-004: Apply power as a lens for influence, control, access and inequality.
- DS-U02-006: Apply systems thinking to connected digital and human systems.
- DS-U03-004: Explain networks and the internet, including connectivity and data transmission.
- DS-IQ-003/004/005: Gather evidence, analyse impacts and implications, and communicate findings.
