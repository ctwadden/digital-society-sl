# School walkthrough and small pilot — not yet run

This review build must be tried on an actual ordinary school Windows computer, the intended student browser/profile and the school network before a classroom-readiness claim. A cloud Chromium result does not replace this step.

## Teacher walkthrough (about one lesson; timing is unmeasured)

Record date, build version, machine model/RAM if known, Windows version, browser/version, screen/zoom, student-profile restrictions and connection. Use anonymous aggregate observations outside the game.

1. Open the intended student hosting address without creating a student account. Check load time and blocked scripts/downloads. The initial private review Site is for the teacher; a student rollout audience/host still needs a decision.
   On a fresh 0.6 campaign, check Jo's prepared one-missing-link lesson, **Find reception's port**, connection and first current booking result. Before explaining the interface, ask the learner to identify their role, who needs help and what they will try first. Confirm the necessary explanatory sentence remains visible with tools open. On an existing save, confirm the actual task resumes; open **Help → Guided connection lesson**, then return without losing the prior attempt or charges.
   Deliberately unplug, observe the stale result, retest, reconnect and retest. Ask the learner what the observations established. Verify the guided explanation and trainer/player distinction in export; an oral explanation remains pending. Complete into the independent equipment station without awarded bootcamp checkpoints.
2. Complete all three free bootcamp stations. Use a demonstration, retry, taught address repair and the new missing-uplink transfer. Ask why changing the IP cannot repair that link; inspect the exact explanation or pending oral arrangement in export. Confirm the demonstration does not count as independent work.
3. Start the clinic. Choose the desktop/tablet/server/wearable route, switch and AP. Use labelled controls to connect actual ports and configure/power services.
4. Test the inherited nurse address error, make a diagnosis, change the address and test again. Unplug a link and confirm the right dependent service fails; reconnect it and recover.
5. Collect seven synthetic home markers, return the wearable, attach the sensor dock, hand off and upload. No medical readings or patient data should appear.
6. Try separate staff roles and nurse-also-books. Both can satisfy the brief. Try open access and blanket denial, and confirm the diagnostic findings explain the different failures.
7. Start teacher-selected full-clinic practice from a saved client, open guides free, and return. Confirm the client configuration and charges remain intact and practice cannot earn payment. Then open a technical guide section in the client job. Confirm the quoted fictional cost and simulated time, reopen it free, then verify the report and final fee. Controls, glossary, diagnostics and accessibility help are free.
8. Type a final sentence and immediately export the evidence. Open both readable and JSON evidence. Compare the latest sentence, actual IP, policy, results, support charges and income with the screen. Oral-review arrangements must remain pending.
9. Reload; export/import progress; try a corrupt file; open a second tab and transfer control. Confirm recovery is understandable and no unsaved work is silently overwritten.
10. Test reset/cancel/export-first and clear game progress on the shared device. Confirm unrelated site data is untouched.
11. Complete the important controls using keyboard only. Check focus, reading order, 200% zoom, narrow layout, reduced motion and the device-list alternative. Test a screen reader if one will be used by the class.

## Decisions for the teacher after walkthrough

- Does delayed return-and-sync fit the wearable task, or should the later mission require a live remote path?
- Do the learning statements and guide wording suit the class's starting knowledge?
- Is the fictional guide tariff motivating or does it discourage necessary help? Adjust before the student pilot if needed.
- Is one bootcamp plus clinic best spread across two lessons?
- Which approved no-account hosting address will students use, with verified limits and school access?

## Four-to-six-student pilot

Include varied confidence and at least one keyboard-only or larger-text route. Observe: first purposeful action, repeated misconceptions, where learners need rescue, recovery after a fault, distinction between physical link/address/access, reasoning for equipment and permissions, and whether the report matches actions. Do not infer understanding from completion, income or hint count. Ask one changed-situation explanation such as “What changes if the nurse needs access outside the building?”

Record time to first purposeful action and first successful reception booking test, number and cause of teacher rescues, whether the current two or three goals are understood, and whether guide cost discourages needed help. These are observation measures, not grades or a preselected speed target. Record aggregate issues and revise the build before wider rollout. No names or new analytics are needed inside the game.

For the 0.6 experience revision, also observe whether students find the Field notebook when needed, distinguish Clinic/Network/List, connect through endpoint selections without precision dragging, and explain a failed test instead of repeatedly trying unrelated settings. Check whether the coherent workplace makes device locations clearer and whether the guided-to-independent transition is understandable. Do not report the redesigned opening as engaging until actual learners have tried it.
