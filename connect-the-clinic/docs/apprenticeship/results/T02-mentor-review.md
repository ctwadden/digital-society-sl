## Reviewed and retained as accurate

The remaining mentor/staff lines are consistent with the current abstraction when read as ordinary dialogue: wrong cable categories do not mutate the network; occupied ports and same-device cable ends are rejected; the model distinguishes association, AP power and Ethernet uplink; `ADDRESS_WRONG_SUBNET`, `ADDRESS_DUPLICATE`, `ADDRESS_RESERVED`, `ADDRESS_OFF_LINK`, `WIFI_NOT_ASSOCIATED`, `AP_OFF`, `PORT_DISCONNECTED`, `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE`, `ACCESS_DENIED`, `CAPABILITY_MISSING`, `DEVICE_MISSING` and `OUTSIDE_COVERAGE` have corresponding causes; a setting change stales earlier tests; and the third visit correctly asks for observation, repair, retest, access checks and an exact saved explanation.

No mentor line claims crimping or router/gateway configuration. Routing is absent from the dialogue, as required. The proposed edits above remove the explicit link-light claim and other unmodelled tactile/visual claims.

## Teaching and privacy boundary to preserve during integration

- Prefer “synthetic booking task,” “workspace,” “service” and “role” over appointments, patients, clinical notes or authenticated individuals.
- An expected `ACCESS_DENIED` is a passing restriction only after the model confirms the physical path, addressing and service. It is not proof of a complete security system.
- Do not change “Saved as you wrote it. Your teacher reads that, not me.” or the oral-review option: the game must preserve learner words and leave quality to teacher judgement.
- Keep mentor help and retries free and reading untimed. The reviewed text contains no fee or time pressure, and `ASK_MENTOR` records `cost: 0` without completing learner checks.
