# Opening Shift — short teacher operating guide

**Teacher review build.** Actual managed school Windows/browser checks and a small student pilot are still required. Test results and remaining limitations are recorded in `../../qa/OPENING_SHIFT_RELEASE.md`.

- New job: https://ctwadden.github.io/digital-society-sl/connect-the-clinic/dist/opening-shift.html
- Earlier taught bootcamp and wearable clinic: https://ctwadden.github.io/digital-society-sl/connect-the-clinic/dist/

Opening Shift is a narrower, more substantial first independent job. It does not mark the earlier bootcamp or wearable mission complete. The earlier game, its evidence and its saved progress remain separate.

## Preparation and first five minutes

Use the existing bootcamp to teach the physical path and static-address task before releasing students into independent work. Opening Shift also provides **Free rehearsal** and the **How-to guide**. Rehearsal is self-directed practice, not a claim that a student has been taught or mastered every concept. The address guide explains `/24` using a different network from the client job.

Start with **Review job**, read the client request, and choose **Begin client job**. Reception already works. The task is to make a mobile nurse workspace work in both exam rooms while preserving reception and making a justified booking-access decision. The phone's optional questions reveal mobility, typing and workflow needs. They do not reveal an unobserved hidden fault.

A useful early observation should be possible within approximately five minutes. This is an unmeasured pilot target, not a real-time limit. An independent shift after preparation is planned for roughly 20–30 minutes; observe beginners before committing lesson time.

## Controls and mission loop

| Tool | What the student does | What counts as evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kit** | Explicitly keep the clinic laptop or choose a tablet; buy/install missing infrastructure; reconsider and return purchased equipment | Actual choice and transactions; remaining client allowance is separate from earnings |
| **Devices** | Open an inspector, check power/settings, apply an address and mask, place equipment and associate Wi-Fi | Actual settings inspected and accepted actions; a visible diagram alone is not a service test |
| **Cables** | Select compatible device ports and a cable; connect or disconnect without precision dragging | Actual endpoint connections and cable inventory |
| **Tests** | Test reception, the nurse, booking workflow and restricted access | Recorded pass/fail, actual reason, location and tested revision |
| **Access** | Keep separated responsibilities or authorize nurses to book; consider unsuitable alternatives | Actual policy and staff/outsider probe consequences; a sensible unchanged policy can be deliberately retained |
| **Handover** | Save four short explanations or identify oral items; review readiness and request handover | Exact learner words, decisions, tests and unresolved teacher judgement |

Use **Room A**, **Room B** and **Run nurse here** to make the two visits explicit. A whole-suite test checks the current room; it cannot invent a visit to the other room. Make the intended access decision before the final tests. A later causal change makes old results stale and requires fresh confirmation.

The scene is supplementary: all required actions have named HTML controls and keyboard access. Neutral cables show installation. A highlighted recorded route comes from an actual test. Read its label and freshness rather than interpreting colour alone.

## Two fixture variants and reasonable alternatives

The default client job begins with an incomplete wireless installation and an inherited nurse address profile that requires investigation. The changed rehearsal begins with suitable addressing and a disconnected AP uplink. Students should discover the cause through inspection/testing. Changing an address cannot supply a missing physical path.

Both retaining the laptop and choosing the tablet can satisfy the brief. The first favors an existing keyboard and lower expenditure; the second favors lighter carrying at additional cost. Both responsible booking policies can satisfy the service requirements, with different work procedures. The game tests functional consequences; the teacher reviews the quality of the student's reason.

The client has 180 fictional equipment credits. An AP costs 40, an uplink cable 4, and the optional tablet 70 in the independent job. Contractor service fee is 120, separate from the equipment allowance. Unused client funds never become profit. Returns before handover refund the actual paid price once. Practice purchases/help have no cost or earnings; this is explicitly labelled.

## Help and academic assessment

All ordinary controls, inspections, diagnostics, accessibility features and reading time are free. Independent-job guide sections cost 5 fictional credits and 3 simulated consultation minutes once per section, capped at 15 credits/9 minutes; all three hint levels and reopening are included. Free rehearsal provides the same guidance without charges. No real countdown runs.

Completion, income, speed and hint use never generate an IB grade or certify mastery. Handover requires explanation presence or a pending oral arrangement; it does not grade prose. Review equipment fit, diagnosis, a relevant retest, stakeholder effects and a limitation through observation, conversation and the export. Official outcome/Evidence Map IDs remain unresolved until supplied and reconciled.

## Save, recovery and shared school computers

Progress is local to the same browser profile and site origin. It does not sync through GitHub or between home and school. Use the progress controls to **download progress** before changing device/browser/profile or clearing data. A progress JSON restores the whole Opening Shift campaign; evidence text/JSON is a teacher record, not a backup.

Importing damaged, oversized, unsupported or inconsistent progress must leave the current attempt untouched. Review a valid replacement before confirming it. Earlier 0.6 progress belongs in the preserved earlier game. New and old namespaces are independent; neither import pretends that the other mission was completed.

Only one tab controls an Opening Shift save at a time. Another tab is read-only until explicit control transfer. Read the save-status message. If automatic storage or the required tab lock is unavailable, the game reports memory/manual-save mode: download work before closing it. A last-good local snapshot provides an additional recovery option when available.

Unsubmitted editor drafts are separate from accepted evidence. Review/save them before handover/export or download the clearly labelled draft rescue. A reset/new practice attempt gets its own ID so old drafts cannot silently become a new answer. Reset of an active client installation preserves paid guide costs and refunds equipment; reset after delivery opens free practice instead of awarding a second fee.

On a shared profile, another student's local attempt may remain. Follow a teacher-managed export-and-clear routine. School logout cleanup, private browsing, storage policies and download restrictions may remove or block local files. The game requests no names, accounts or patient information; remind students to keep identifying details out of free-text explanations.

## Walkthrough and pilot decisions

First, play the published default job all the way to evidence export. Then try the changed rehearsal and ask why an IP edit would not fix its physical fault. Check one alternate equipment/policy plan, the damaged-import message and a second tab. Finally observe three to five beginners on actual school devices: first-use confusion, time to a useful observation, willingness to seek help, keyboard/zoom use and quality of transfer explanations.

Teacher decisions still needed are whether to retain the fictional help tariff, how much preparation this class needs, realistic lesson timing, acceptable written/oral evidence, and exact curriculum/Evidence Map identifiers. The source-linked I-can map and optional Systems, Power, Values and ethics and Health lenses are in `LEARNING_DESIGN.md`. Economics remains a reflection on incentives; earning game credits is not evidence of whole-chapter understanding.
