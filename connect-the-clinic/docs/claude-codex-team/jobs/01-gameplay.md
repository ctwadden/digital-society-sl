# Codex job 01 — Gameplay and mentor narrative

## Assignment

You are the gameplay specialist working for Claude Code, the department head. Turn the corrected apprenticeship into an enjoyable sequence of meaningful actions. Satisfy **R1, R3, R4 and R5**. Read `docs/claude-codex-team/WORKER_CONTRACT.md`; Claude supplies the task packet with the exact baseline, slice and writable paths. Do not build a second game or delegate further.

## Skills

Read `learning-simulation-design` when locally available. Use `delegate` only for its bounded contract and reporting discipline; remain a worker. If these skills are absent, follow the common worker contract and this brief. Report availability without claiming installation.

## Inputs

Read `docs/CLAUDE_CODE_HANDOFF.md`, the assigned existing model/API contracts, supplied source notes and current playable entry. Use the Aspire analysis as historical design evidence, not copied content or proof of a complete playthrough. Confirm what the model can actually represent.

## Owned outputs

Proposed defaults are `docs/apprenticeship/GAMEPLAY.md` and `dist/apprenticeship/story.js`. These are not permission to edit: only the task packet's activated allowlist grants ownership. Shared programme, entry, model and interface files remain Claude-owned unless explicitly leased.

## Deliverable

Author the opening and assigned visit as playable beats: meet the mentor, prepare equipment, arrive with a purpose, make a choice, inspect and operate something, observe a diagnostic, receive relevant advice, repair and see a staff task resume. Specify the player's action, model observation, mentor response and human consequence for each beat. Keep the clinic scene primary and messages short.

Design three meaningfully different supervised visits with decreasing unsolicited help, followed by the earned-truck transition. Implement only the assigned slice. Include plausible mistakes, recovery, optional demonstration, returning-player entry and free help. Name stable message/phase IDs and the exact event or selector permitting each success message. Ask the lead for missing contract fields; never invent parallel success flags.

## Concrete checks

Walk through a successful route, wrong cable or port, failed test, targeted advice and repair. Check that demonstrations cannot earn practice evidence, dialogue cannot advance unearned progress and staff consequences require current service evidence. Establish target pacing as a hypothesis until beginner observation supports it. Report a design walkthrough separately from actual gameplay testing.

## Budget and result

Complete one bounded initial attempt and one focused repair if requested. After the same criterion fails twice, return the cause and changed approach to Claude. Return `complete`, `partial` or `blocked`, changed paths, observed checks, uncertainties and integration instructions using the common result format. Do not publish.
