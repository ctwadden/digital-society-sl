CASE BRIEF · BRITISH LIBRARY CYBERATTACK

Inquiry question
How should a public knowledge institution balance open access, security, cost and transparency after a disruptive cyberattack?

Verified starting facts
- The British Library says a ransomware attack began on 28 October 2023.
- Its incident review reports that attackers copied about 600 GB of files.
- Core digital infrastructure was damaged, while the physical and digital collections remained secure.
- The UK Information Commissioner's Office said in April 2025 that the incident escalated because multi-factor authentication was not enabled on an administrator account.
- Recovery has been phased; some services returned before others.

Important caution
Do not turn these facts into a simple story of one cause or one villain. Separate what a source directly establishes from your inference. Ask whose interests each source represents and what evidence would change your judgement.

Source roles to locate
1. Institution: British Library incident review or recovery update.
2. Regulator: Information Commissioner's Office statement.
3. Technical public-interest authority: National Cyber Security Centre analysis.
